DreamersJoy — 6x4 landscape cards, front and back

18 designs:
  16 occasion cards  (Happy Birthday ... blank)
   2 care cards      (one for arrangements in a vessel, one for hand-tieds)

PRINT SPEC — give this to VistaPrint / Moo / Zazzle:
  Finished (trim) size   6 x 4 in, LANDSCAPE
  Document size          6.25 x 4.25 in
  Bleed                  0.125 in all four sides
  Safety margin          0.25 in inside the trim
  Colour                 RGB
  Sides                  2 (page 1 = front, page 2 = back)

ORDER ONE AS A PROOF FIRST. These are RGB; the vendor converts to CMYK and
the navy and gold can print a shade flatter than they look on screen.

THE TWO CARE CARDS ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE.
  care-arrangement  goes with anything built in a vessel. It says DO NOT recut.
  care-bouquet      goes with a hand-tied. It says recut every stem.
Sending the wrong one either dismantles an arrangement or leaves a bouquet
unable to drink. Keep them in separate piles.
