DreamersJoy — 7x5 landscape cards, front and back

18 designs:
  16 occasion cards  (Happy Birthday ... blank)
   2 care cards      (one for arrangements in a vessel, one for hand-tieds)

PRINT SPEC
  Finished (trim) size   7 x 5 in, LANDSCAPE
  Document size          7.25 x 5.25 in
  Bleed                  0.125 in all four sides
  Safety margin          0.25 in inside the trim
  Colour                 RGB
  Sides                  2 (page 1 = front, page 2 = back)

STAPLES — Same Day Greeting Cards
  Card Type        Same-day card
  Product Options  Landscape, 7" x 5"
  Upload the PDF as-is. Their canvas reads 7.25 x 5.25 with the safe area
  drawn 0.25in inside the trim, which is exactly what these are built to.
  Do NOT use "Scale to fit" — it resizes artwork that is already correct and
  will pull the frame out of true. If the preview looks right at 100%, it is
  right.

ORDER ONE AS A PROOF FIRST. These are RGB; the printer converts to CMYK and
the navy and gold can come out a shade flatter than they look on screen.

THE TWO CARE CARDS ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE.
  care-arrangement  goes with anything built in a vessel. It says DO NOT recut.
  care-bouquet      goes with a hand-tied. It says recut every stem.
Sending the wrong one either dismantles an arrangement or leaves a bouquet
unable to drink. Keep them in separate piles.
