  16 occasion cards   7 x 5 landscape   (Happy Birthday ... blank)
   2 care cards       3.5 x 2           (one for arrangements, one for hand-tieds)

  Upload the PDF as-is. Do NOT use "Scale to fit" — it resizes artwork that is
  already correct and pulls the frame out of true. If it looks right at 100%,
  it is right.


CARE CARDS — 3.5 x 2, from VistaPrint
  Finished (trim) size   3.5 x 2 in  (standard business card)
  Document size          3.75 x 2.25 in
  Bleed                  0.125 in all four sides
  Safety margin          0.125 in inside the trim

  A care note is reference, not a gift, so it does not need to match the
  occasion card. Business-card size is by far the cheapest way to print these
  and it tucks into the envelope beside the card.

  The copy is four points, not six. Six will not fit at a size anyone can
  read. The two that had to survive the edit are the two that actually
  change the outcome: keep the water topped up, and — depending on the
  card — do or do not recut.


BOTH: colour is RGB, two sides (page 1 = front, page 2 = back).
ORDER ONE AS A PROOF FIRST. The printer converts to CMYK and the navy and
gold can come out a shade flatter than they look on screen.


THE TWO CARE CARDS ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE.
  care-arrangement  goes with anything built in a vessel. It says DO NOT recut.
  care-bouquet      goes with a hand-tied. It says recut every stem.
Sending the wrong one either dismantles an arrangement or leaves a bouquet
unable to drink. Keep them in separate piles.
