DreamersJoy Floral Studio — print assets

build-sheets/   One PDF per arrangement on the site (19).
                Product photo, palette, stem list with a month-by-month
                availability strip, vessel and mechanics, where to source,
                and the order of work. Two pages each.

occasion-cards/ Sixteen enclosure cards, 6x4 inches LANDSCAPE, front and
                back. Page 1 is the front, page 2 is the back.
                Same proportions and typography as the Mother's Day card,
                so a stack of these reads as one family.

                PRINT SPEC — give this to VistaPrint / Moo / Zazzle:
                  Finished (trim) size   6 x 4 in, landscape
                  Document size          6.25 x 4.25 in
                  Bleed                  0.125 in on all four sides
                  Safety margin          0.25 in inside the trim
                  Colour                 RGB
                  Sides                  2 (front and back)

                The cream runs into the bleed on purpose, so the trim can
                wander slightly without leaving a white edge.

                ORDER ONE AS A PROOF FIRST. These are RGB; the vendor
                converts to CMYK at their end, and the navy (#334155) and
                gold (#8B7355) can print a shade flatter than they look on
                screen. Prove one design before committing to sixteen.

                The back is deliberately empty — that is where the
                handwritten message goes.
