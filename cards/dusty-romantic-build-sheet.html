Before you buy anything

It isn't a vase

The product photo is a low, wide footed bowl — roughly 8–9 inches across, 4–5 tall, stone or matte ceramic. A compote, not a vase. If you set this in a tall vase it becomes a different piece and the silhouette collapses.

Which means mechanics: a chicken-wire ball taped in with waterproof tape, or a pin frog. Water to just under the rim.

Shopping

Grouped by stop, not by flower

About 28–36 stems for Signature scale. Tick as you go.

Farmstand or farmers market

Late September is peak dahlia. This is where the moody end of the palette lives — nothing at a supermarket will match it.

Trader Joe's

Reliable for the soft mass. Pick by colour and walk past anything clear or bright — one true pink breaks the whole palette.

Whole Foods, or a retail florist

The gap-filler stop. Skip it entirely if the first two came through.

Check at home first

Find the shortfall today, while there's still time to fix it.

Build

Thursday evening

  1. Condition first

    Cut on an angle, strip everything below the waterline, two hours minimum in cool water somewhere dark. Non-negotiable if it's travelling Friday.

  2. Wire the bowl

    Chicken-wire ball, waterproof tape across in a cross, water just under the rim.

  3. Greenery sets the shape

    Low and wide. Establish the one long trailing line down a single side — the asymmetry is the whole silhouette.

  4. Roses next

    Asymmetric cluster, varied heights, faces turned differently. Never a dome.

  5. Dahlias recessed

    Set slightly deeper than the roses. That's where the depth comes from.

  6. Lisianthus to bridge

    Between the mass and the air.

  7. Scabiosa last, riding high

    Above the mass on their own stems. Resist tucking them in.

  8. Texture into the gaps

    Fill the negative space without closing it. Stop earlier than feels finished.
