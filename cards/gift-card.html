Enclosure card · 6 × 4 in

Click any line to edit it. The card is set to the same cream, navy and gold as your Mother's Day card, so a stack of these reads as one family.

  1. Type the occasion and the message.
  2. Print with ⌘ P — choose 4 × 6 photo paper, or Save as PDF and send it to a print shop.
  3. Set scale to 100%, not "fit to page", or the frame shifts off-centre.
  4. Turn on background graphics in the print dialog, or the cream drops out and prints white.

Reusable — retype the two lines for the baby shower favours, or for any order. Nothing is saved, so each print starts from whatever is on screen.
