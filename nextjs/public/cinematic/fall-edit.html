Fall Season 2026

The Fall Edit

Modern, flowy autumn design in three color-led palettes. Centerpieces in three scales and hand-tied bouquets, composed with intentional negative space and long, draping movement.

The Method

Color First, Then Flowers

We design within a defined palette system. This season’s three palettes are drawn from the Pantone® Fashion Color Trend Report for Autumn/Winter 2026. The palette is set first — then we source the best of what’s blooming that week and compose to it. No two pieces are identical. Every piece is unmistakably its palette.
